What's next

From tools for me to apps for everyone.

Everything on this site was built for me first. That's on purpose: if I use it every day, I find out fast what works. The next step is taking the things that hold up and making them solid enough for other people to rely on.

How I pick what to build

It has to bug me

Every project starts with something I ran into myself: a subscription I didn't want, a tab I kept switching to, a problem at a festival.

One at a time

I finish one project to a version I'm happy with before starting the next. Ideas go on a list until it's their turn.

Use it, then share it

I run it myself first. Only what I keep using gets cleaned up and shared.
