A fan-made companion app for Dreamstate SoCal 2026 at the Queen Mary in Long Beach. Hear the artists before you go, plan your sets, and find your way around, even with no signal.

The story

The problem

The Dreamstate lineup was full of artists I half knew. The set times came as a flat image, there was no easy way to hear everyone before the weekend, and at a sold-out show on the water, cell service is the first thing to go.

How I thought about it

Three jobs, in order: listen, plan, go. Hear the artists before you get there. Pick your sets and keep the plan on your phone. Then find your way around once you're inside. Everything had to work one-handed, on a phone, in a crowd.

How I got there

I matched every artist on the lineup against my Apple Music and MP3 libraries and sorted the tracks by set time. Then came the set time grid, the map, a queue player and live timing. I shared it on my Instagram story so friends could use it too.

What the festival taught me

On day one, with weak signal, the app loaded blank until I switched to airplane mode. The phone kept waiting on a network that wasn't really there. I rebuilt it to open from the saved copy first and check for updates in the background, and added a notice so people know it works offline.

Playlists

863 tracks, sorted by when they play

I matched every artist on the lineup against my own music: 706 tracks from Apple Music and 157 from my MP3 library. The list follows the weekend in order, day by day and stage by stage, so you can scroll it like the festival itself.

Every track has a 30 second preview, an add to queue button, and links to open it in Apple Music or Spotify. Search by artist or title, filter by day and stage, or turn on autoplay and let a whole set run. Tags call out remixes, features and tracks that came from my MP3 library.

How it works

Listen, plan, go.

  1. 1. ListenTap play on any track for a 30 second preview. The player stays docked at the bottom while you browse. Build a queue, or turn on autoplay to roll through a whole set hands-free.
  2. 2. Find your setsPick a day and a stage, like Friday and The Void. Each stage keeps its own color everywhere in the app, so Dream, Sequence, Void and Vision are easy to spot.
  3. 3. PlanOpen Set Times and tap the star on the sets you want. Your picks are saved on your phone and stay highlighted on the grid.
  4. 4. GoDuring the festival, live timing turns on by itself: who's on each stage now, minutes left, who's next, and a red line for the current time. It runs off your phone's clock, so it works without signal.
  5. 5. Save it to your home screenIn Safari, tap Share then Add to Home Screen (in Chrome, the menu then Add to Home screen). It opens full screen like an app and loads from the saved copy, even with no service.

Tap play for a preview, build a queue, or open the song in Apple Music or Spotify.
