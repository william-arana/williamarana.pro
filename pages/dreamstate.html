What it does

Playlists by set time

Every song in my Apple Music and MP3 libraries by an artist on the lineup, sorted by when they play. Filter by day and stage.

30 second previews

Tap play for a preview, build a queue, or open the song in Apple Music or Spotify.

Set times and a plan

Interactive schedule for both days. Star the sets you want and your plan stays saved on your phone.

Live timing

Shows what's playing now and what's up next across every stage.

Festival map

Zoomable map of the Queen Mary grounds, stages included.

Works with no signal

Installs like an app and opens from its saved copy first, so it still loads when the festival network is down.
